# GallopWire demo note
- **Set up:** plug in a MIDI keyboard and open https://gallopwire.vercel.app in Chrome or Edge. Tap to start audio and pick your keyboard under MIDI in. Use BPM 96, key E minor, chords `Em Em C D Em Am B7 Em`.
- **Play:** press ▶ Play. With your left hand hold low power-chord shapes around E2-E3 (E B E, C G C, D A D…): Neon Gallop plays the root on every beat and two muted stabs after it while the Neon Whistle holds the top note of each chord. Ride the **mod wheel** (CC1) for more surf tremolo, push **CC71** to bit-crush the twang into neon grit, sweep **CC74** brightness darker for the verse, bring the whistle up with **expression/CC11**, and dip the **pitch bend** for a whammy sag on the last note.
- **Suggested patch: "High Noon on Neon Street":**
  - Voice Baritone Twang, brightness 55%, attack 3 ms, release 250 ms
  - Recipe Neon Gallop, ring 1.2 steps, strum 12 ms, surf tremolo 25%, neon crush 25%, whistle 55%, drums Gallop (hoof clops)
  - Bars 1-4 Neon Gallop, bars 5-6 Bounty Run (chase), bars 7-8 Duel at Dusk with sustain held and crush at 60%.
  - Mix: twang 0.85, whistle 0.5, mouth harp 0.55, bass 0.75, kit 0.65
- **Record:** press ● Rec, play 2 passes, press Rec again to keep it, then Export WAV + MIDI and drop both on bar 1.
