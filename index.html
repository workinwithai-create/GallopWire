GALLOPWIRE

A playable cyber-western instrument for your MIDI keys. Hold a chord and the gallop engine rides it on a synth baritone twang (gallop stabs, octave twangs, tremolo picking, surf tremolo, neon bit-crush, slap echo) over a neon whistle, a mouth harp, desert bass and a hoof-clop kit, on the grid of your BPM and key.

Tap to start audio.

1 · Your loop

Your BPM, key and chords are saved in this browser and used for playback and export. Changing the key transposes the chords and your take. The gallop lives on 16 sixteenths per 4/4 bar, so exports sit on a plain DAW grid.

2 · Play

bar – · beat –

Your MIDI keyboard plays the keys voice right away and never starts the transport. Stopped, you hear the raw twang so you can audition voicings. Playing, the gallop engine rides whatever you hold (or your take, or the chord of the bar). Fallback keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; (Z / X = octave).

3 · Gallop engine

16 sixteenth steps per bar. Codes: R low root twang · G gallop stab (muted chord) · O octave twang · T tremolo-picked top note · S strummed chord · H hold · - rest. Click a step to cycle.

5 · MIDI + audio

Controls: notes + velocity · CC64 sustain · CC1 mod wheel = surf tremolo depth · CC74 = brightness · CC71 = neon crush · CC11 expression / CC2 breath = whistle level · pitch bend ±2 (whammy) · CC123 all notes off. MIDI out mirrors what you play and the gallop engine (ch 1), bass (ch 3), mouth harp (ch 4) and kit (ch 10). No audio input is used.

6 · Export (drops on bar 1 of your DAW)

48 kHz / 24-bit stereo, exactly your bars at your BPM, downbeat at sample 0, twang ring-out, slap echo and canyon tail folded into the start so it loops, peak ≤ −1 dBFS. MIDI: tempo, 4/4, key, one track per chair (Baritone Twang, Neon Whistle, Mouth Harp, Desert Bass, Gallop Kit).

Every sound in GallopWire is synthesized in your browser and named as a synth. No samples, no accounts, no tracking. © 2026 WorkinWithAI.
